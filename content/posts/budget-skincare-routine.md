You do not need a 10-step routine. You need four things done in the right order, using products that actually match your skin — and that's it. Here's the lazy version, sorted by skin type, using nine budget-friendly products.

## The 4 jobs a routine actually needs to do

1. **Cleanse** — get rid of the day's buildup
2. **Moisturize** — keep your skin barrier intact
3. **Protect** — sunscreen, every single morning
4. **Treat (optional)** — a serum, only if you actually want one

Everything below slots into one of those four jobs. Skip whatever doesn't apply to you.

## If your skin is oily or acne-prone

**Morning:** cleanse, sunscreen
**Night:** cleanse, moisturize only if your skin actually needs it

Don't skip moisturizer just because your skin is oily — if it feels tight after cleansing, it still needs one. You don't need a serum here. Piling actives onto acne-prone skin to "complete the routine" usually just irritates it more.
