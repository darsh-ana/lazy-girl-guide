A skincare routine doesn't need ten steps or a five-figure haul to actually work. It needs three things done consistently — cleanse, moisturize, protect — using products that match your skin. Below is a full routine for every skin type, each one landing under ₹1000 total, with the reasoning for why each product is actually in there and not just filler.

## Dry skin — approx. ₹932 total

**Morning:** cleanse → moisturize → sunscreen
**Night:** cleanse → moisturize

{{product:cetaphil-face-wash}}

Dry skin loses moisture fast, so the cleanser's only job here is to clean without stripping whatever moisture is left. Cetaphil is formulated specifically to not disrupt the skin barrier, which matters more for dry skin than any other type — a harsh cleanser here undoes everything the moisturizer after it is trying to do.

{{product:cerave-moisturizer}}

This is the step doing the real work. CeraVe's formula has three ceramides, which are the actual lipids your skin barrier is made of — dry skin is often just a barrier that's lost too many of them. Hyaluronic acid on top pulls in water so the cream isn't just sitting on the surface.

Add the Derma Co sunscreen from the sunscreen section below to complete the morning routine.

**Total: ₹344 (cleanser) + ₹289 (moisturizer) + ₹299 (sunscreen) ≈ ₹932**

**Optional add-on:** if you want brightening on top of hydration, the Hyphen serum is built for exactly that combination.

{{product:hyphen-serum}}

It's not required — dry skin looks fine on cleanser and moisturizer alone. This is for if you specifically want an extra step targeting dullness.

## Oily or acne-prone skin — approx. ₹787 total

**Morning:** cleanse → sunscreen
**Night:** cleanse → moisturize if needed

{{product:minimalist-salicylic-wash}}

Salicylic acid is oil-soluble, so it can actually get into a clogged pore instead of just sitting on top of the skin like most cleansers. The LHA alongside it helps with gentler exfoliation without over-drying, which is the usual failure mode of "acne" face washes.

Pair it with the Derma Co sunscreen below for mornings.

**Total: ₹199 (cleanser) + ₹299 (sunscreen) ≈ ₹498** — add ₹289 for the CeraVe moisturizer only on nights your skin feels tight, bringing it to **≈ ₹787**.

Don't skip moisturizer out of fear it'll make you oilier — skin that's stripped of moisture often overproduces oil to compensate, which makes acne worse, not better. You don't need a serum on top of this. Adding actives to an already-active cleanser is how irritation happens, not how routines get better.

## Normal skin — approx. ₹643 total

**Morning:** cleanse → sunscreen
**Night:** cleanse → moisturize if it feels dry

{{product:cetaphil-face-wash}}

Normal skin doesn't need anything corrective — the cleanser's only job is to not mess up what's already working, which is exactly what a gentle, barrier-safe wash like this is for.

**Total: ₹344 (cleanser) + ₹299 (sunscreen) ≈ ₹643**

**Optional add-on:** if you want a brightening step for uneven tone, the Vitamin C serum is built for that specifically.

{{product:minimalist-vitamin-c}}

This is genuinely optional. Normal skin doesn't need correcting — this is only for someone who wants to actively target dullness or tone, not a routine requirement.

## Combination skin — approx. ₹932 total

**Morning:** cleanse → sunscreen
**Night:** cleanse → moisturize where it's dry

{{product:cetaphil-face-wash}}

A gentle, non-stripping cleanser works here because combination skin's biggest problem is usually a routine that's too harsh for the dry parts and not targeted enough for the oily parts. Starting gentle everywhere is safer than starting aggressive.

{{product:cerave-moisturizer}}

Apply this only where your skin actually feels dry — usually the cheeks — rather than all over. Combination skin doesn't need two different products for two zones, just one applied selectively.

**Total: ₹344 (cleanser) + ₹289 (moisturizer) + ₹299 (sunscreen) ≈ ₹932**

If your T-zone gets oily or congested, swap in the salicylic acid wash on those days instead of making it your everyday cleanser.
